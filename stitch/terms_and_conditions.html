02.

User Accounts & Verification

To register for civil service, engineering, medical, or administrative exam cohorts (e.g., UPSC CSE, State PSC, SSC CGL), you must complete two-factor registration.

check_circle
OTP Telecommunication Verification

Every account is tethered to a distinct primary mobile phone number authenticated via cryptographically signed One-Time Passwords (OTPs) alongside a verified institutional email.

lock
Single-Device Active Session Policy

To safeguard course equity and prevent unauthorized proxy attendance, each student profile is limited to one active web instance and one registered mobile device. Concurrent logins prompt instant termination of prior sessions.

shield
Credential Confidentiality

The enrolled candidate is entirely responsible for transactions and test attempts executed under their registered ID. Unauthorized delegation to peer aspirants constitutes a breach of honor code.

Course Access & Content Licensing

Upon successful enrollment and clearance of fee tranches, The Eduverse grants the student a revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable, limited personal license to:

  • Stream on-demand recorded syllabus lectures and join live doubt clearing sessions for the enrolled batch duration.
  • Download curated Daily Practice Papers (DPPs), faculty lecture transcripts, and mind-maps strictly for personal offline study.
  • Participate in national All-India Rank (AIR) test series, simulated time-bound prelims, and subjective answer-key evaluations.
