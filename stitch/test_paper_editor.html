With reference to the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act, 2003 and its subsequent amendments, consider the following statements:

1. The original Act mandated the Central Government to eliminate the revenue deficit completely by March 31, 2008.

2. The N.K. Singh Committee (2016) on the FRBM framework recommended a target debt-to-GDP ratio of 60% by 2023, with a 40% limit for the Central Government and 20% for State Governments.

3. The 'Escape Clause' under the amended FRBM Act allows deviation from the fiscal deficit path up to 1.5% of GDP under exigencies like war or collapse of agriculture.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct Option: A (Statements 1 and 2 are correct).

Statement 1 is correct: The FRBM Act, 2003 originally envisaged that the revenue deficit would be brought down to zero by March 31, 2008. The target was subsequently deferred through subsequent finance acts.

Statement 2 is correct: The N.K. Singh Committee recommended transitioning the anchor of fiscal policy from fiscal deficit to public debt, prescribing a combined debt-to-GDP ratio of 60% (40% Central, 20% State) to be achieved by FY2023.

Statement 3 is incorrect: Under Section 4(2) of the amended FRBM Act, the Central Government is allowed to deviate from the fiscal deficit target by an amount not exceeding 0.5% of GDP (not 1.5%) on grounds of national security, act of war, or serious collapse of agriculture.
